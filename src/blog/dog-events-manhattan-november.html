Manhattan dog events this November: a guide from your Stuyvesant Town dog walkers

Pupsgiving dinners, adoption days, breed meetups and holiday markets. Here's what's on for Manhattan dogs and their people in November 2026.

By Alvin Daniel Nov 9, 2026 4 min read

Manhattan has a full calendar of dog events this November. The highlights are the Pupsgiving dinners at Boris & Horton on Avenue A (Nov 15 and 21), free-to-attend adoption days from Animal Care Centers of NYC on the Upper West Side (Nov 14 and 15), and dog-friendly programs at the AKC Museum of the Dog (Nov 20 and 21). The Union Square Holiday Market is also about to open, right across 14th Street from our neighborhood.

What is Pupsgiving?

Pupsgiving is a sit-down Thanksgiving dinner for people and their dogs at Boris & Horton, the dog-friendly café on Avenue A in the East Village. According to the event listing, every dog gets its own Thanksgiving meal. Seats are ticketed, so check Boris & Horton for prices and availability before you go.

Can I bring my dog to the Union Square Holiday Market?

The market usually opens in Union Square Park in mid-November and runs until Christmas Eve. The 2026 dates hadn't been confirmed when we wrote this, so check Urbanspace's website before you go. Guides report that leashed dogs are welcome under the park's normal rules, but the market's operator hasn't published a dog policy. The Union Square Dog Run, at 15th Street and Union Square West, is right next to it, so you can stop there before or after shopping.

Bryant Park's Winter Village shops are open through Jan 3. The park doesn't publish a dog policy for the shops either, so check before you bring your dog, and keep in mind the crowds get heavy on weekends.

Where can I adopt a dog in Manhattan this month?

Animal Care Centers of NYC holds free-to-attend mobile adoption events on Saturday, Nov 14 at 1677 Amsterdam Avenue and Sunday, Nov 15 at 563 Columbus Avenue, both from noon to 4pm. Adoption fees apply. If you do bring a dog home, remember that every NYC dog needs a license: $8.50 a year if your dog is spayed or neutered, and $34 a year if not.

What about the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade?

The 100th parade is on Thursday, Nov 26, starting at 8:30am. Most dogs are better off at home that morning. Expect packed sidewalks along the route, loud marching bands, street closures and crowded trains. A calm early walk close to home is the kinder plan.
