Our story

We grew up here. This community is home.

Dog Walkin.com didn't start as a business plan. It started with a love for animals and a deep connection to Stuyvesant Town — one of the most unique neighborhoods in New York City.

We know these paths because we've walked them our whole lives. We know the doormen, the dog runs, the best spots to let a nervous pup decompress. That kind of knowledge isn't something you build overnight — it's something you grow up with.

That's why every client feels like a neighbor, and every pet feels like part of the family.

Meet your walker

David

Lead Walker & Founder · Stuyvesant Town Native

David has spent his entire life in Stuyvesant Town, and his entire adult life working with dogs. He brings a calm, knowledgeable energy that even the most anxious animals respond to — because he genuinely understands canine behavior, not just routines.

He's not just walking your dog. He's watching how they interact with other animals, reading their body language, and making real-time decisions that keep every walk safe and enjoyable. He's the kind of person your dog will wait by the door for.

David leads every client relationship personally. When you book with Dog Walkin.com, you're working with someone who lives in your building, walks your block, and cares about your pet the way a neighbor would.
